# Medical Card Types & Written-Style Standards

This document defines the 7 High-Yield Medical Pillars and the model answer formatting standards used by `transcriber-anki`.

---

## 1. The 7 Medical Pillars

### `def` — Definitions & Diagnostic Criteria
- **Focus:** Core concept definitions, diagnostic criteria, legal/toxicological concepts.
- **Answer Rule:** Clear 1–3 line statement covering definition, tissue/organ affected, and clinical consequence.

### `types` — Classifications & Subtypes
- **Focus:** Structured classification schemes, stages, or histological types.
- **Answer Rule:** Numbered list (1–5 items) with concise subtitles.

### `mechanism` — Pathophysiology & Mechanisms
- **Focus:** Biochemical action, receptor binding, cellular necrosis differences (e.g. coagulative vs liquefactive).
- **Answer Rule:** Step-by-step causal chain ($\rightarrow$) or comparative 2-part summary.

### `signs` — Clinical Picture, Triads & Buzzwords
- **Focus:** Pathognomonic clinical signs, unusual odors, pupil changes, urine colors.
- **Answer Rule:** Bulleted buzzwords with short clinical explanation in parentheses.

### `TTT` — Treatment Protocols, Antidotes & Contraindications
- **Focus:** Emergency ABCs, specific antidotes, dosage timing, decontamination rules, and **Absolute Contraindications**.
- **Answer Rule:** Numbered intervention steps followed by a dedicated contraindications warning block.

### `complications` — Timelines & Causes of Death
- **Focus:** Immediate vs intermediate vs late complications, causes of death structured by time (Day 1, Week 1, Week 3).
- **Answer Rule:** Chronologically grouped bullet points.

### `past_exams` — Past Exam MCQs & Traps
- **Focus:** Verbatim past exam questions and university assessment items.
- **Answer Rule:** Correct option identified with concise mark-scheme rationale.

---

## 2. Egyptian Written Exam Model Answer Rules

- **Keyword Dominance:** Every bullet point must begin with a strong, capitalized keyword (e.g. `1. **Airway Protection:** ...`, `2. **Dilution Therapy:** ...`).
- **Length Constraint:** 1–5 words per sub-point. Never use long essay paragraphs on the back of cards.
- **Separation of Warnings:** All fatal errors / contraindications must be highlighted in the contraindications block (`⚠️ Absolute Contraindications`).
